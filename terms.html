Terms of Service

Effective 19 November 2026.

1. What JARVIS is

JARVIS Content Manager ("JARVIS") is a personal desktop assistant that runs only on its owner's own computer. It is a private, single-user tool. It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else, and there is no sign-up.

2. Who may use it

Only the owner of the computer it is installed on uses JARVIS, with their own TikTok account. Nobody else is invited to connect an account.

3. TikTok

JARVIS uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API ("Upload to TikTok"). Videos are sent to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts; the owner decides in the TikTok app whether and how to post them. Use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. The owner is responsible for having the rights to every video and sound they post.

4. No warranty

JARVIS is provided as is, without any warranty. It may stop working if TikTok changes its services.

5. Stopping use

The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time by removing the TikTok sign-in (tiktok-oauth) in JARVIS under Settings → Credentials, or remove JARVIS's access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps).
