Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

JARVIS is a personal desktop assistant that runs on its owner's own Windows computer. It is a private, single-user tool: it is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, and is not sold.

What it does with TikTok

  • The owner signs in to their own TikTok account through TikTok's official sign-in page and approves access.
  • When the owner chooses a finished video, JARVIS sends it to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft (TikTok's "Upload to TikTok" feature). The owner then reviews, edits and posts it in the TikTok app.
  • JARVIS reads the account's basic profile (display name and username) only to label the connection and link to the owner's posts.
  • JARVIS does not post publicly by itself, does not read other people's data, and does not message, follow, like or comment.
