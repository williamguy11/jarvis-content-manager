Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026.

Who this covers

JARVIS Content Manager ("JARVIS") is a personal desktop assistant used only by its owner on their own Windows computer. It has no other users.

What JARVIS receives from TikTok

  • Access and refresh tokens, issued by TikTok when the owner signs in and approves access.
  • The account's open ID, display name and username (scopes user.info.basic and user.info.profile), used only to label the connection and link to the owner's own posts.
  • Upload and processing status for videos the owner chooses to send (scope video.upload).

Where it is kept

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in Windows Credential Manager. They are never written to logs, shown on screen, or sent anywhere except back to TikTok's own servers. There is no JARVIS server and no cloud copy.

Sharing

JARVIS does not sell, rent or share any data, and contains no advertising or analytics trackers. Videos are sent only to TikTok, and only when the owner chooses to.

Deleting data

Removing the TikTok sign-in (tiktok-oauth) in JARVIS under Settings → Credentials deletes the stored tokens. Access can also be removed in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps).

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
